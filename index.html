Brewshare Support

Coffee, shared.

Brewshare is a community app for sharing coffee photos and recipes — vote on favorites, browse what's popular or nearby, and discover new recipes from other coffee lovers.

Need help?

A dedicated support inbox is on the way. In the meantime, if you run into a problem or have feedback, please leave a review on the App Store or check back here for an updated contact method.

Privacy

Brewshare stores photos, recipes, and votes you submit via Apple's CloudKit. Location is only attached to a submission if you explicitly choose to share it.
